Design system · v1

Sentinel — surveillance console

A single dark operator theme for Home CCTV Hub. Calm surfaces, one signal accent, status that never relies on colour alone, and a typography scale tuned for 5-second scanning of camera names, times and priorities.

Dark only System fonts WCAG AA body text
Principles 4 rules
01
Operate, don’t decorate
Every pixel earns its place. Surveillance chrome should disappear behind the picture, the time and the state.
02
Status is never colour-only
Every online/offline, sent/failed, enabled/disabled signal pairs its colour with a dot shape and a word.
03
Dense, then calm
Compact 38 px controls and 13 px body text, separated by a strict 4 px spacing rhythm and generous card padding.
04
One accent, few alarms
Krypton teal is action and live signal. Amber and red are reserved for warnings and failures — never decoration.
Colour surfaces · ink · signal

Four surface steps create depth without shadows. Two ink steps plus muted/faint carry all text. Teal is the only accent; status colours are semantic.

Accessibility contract non-negotiable
  • ▪ Focus visible on every interactive element: 2 px teal ring, 2 px offset.
  • ▪ Status text accompanies every colour (Online / Offline / Checking).
  • ▪ Tap targets ≥ 36 px on desktop, ≥ 44 px on mobile primary controls.
  • ▪ Reduced motion: live pulse and shimmer disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • ▪ Contrast: body ≥ 4.5:1; non-text UI boundaries ≥ 3:1 against adjacent colours.
  • ▪ Semantics: one h1 per page; named landmarks; tables with headers; no icon-only nav in the tested app.

Do

  • Keep the exact strings and roles the Playwright suite asserts.
  • Use aria-current="page" for the active destination.
  • Announce async results with role="status" / role="alert".

Don’t

  • Don’t introduce a webfont or icon CDN — CSP blocks it.
  • Don’t render time/random values during SSR (hydration warning).
  • Don’t make the calendar a fixed panel or change the Date trigger text.
